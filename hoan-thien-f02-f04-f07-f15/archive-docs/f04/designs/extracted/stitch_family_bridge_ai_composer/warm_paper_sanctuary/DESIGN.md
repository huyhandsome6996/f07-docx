## Brand & Style

The design system creates a calm, deliberate sanctuary for students and caregivers to articulate sensitive thoughts without pressure, judgment, or clinical sterility. Built around the tactile metaphor of fine stationery and warm craft paper, the interface rejects aggressive gamification, synthetic neon accents, and faux-clinical "mental health chatbot" tropes.

The emotional tone balances respectful listening, safety, and autonomy:
- **Respectful & Consensual:** Every interaction reinforces voluntary expression. Suggestions from assistive intelligence are presented as gentle, editable drafts—never prescriptive corrections or automated auto-fills.
- **Warm Tactility:** Soft organic tones replicate natural parchment, grounding the digital writing desk into an intimate, stress-reducing physical space.
- **Privacy-Forward Serenity:** Interface chrome remains quiet, allowing typography and spacious margins to protect the user's emotional bandwidth.

## Colors

The palette derives warmth from terracotta, unbleached cotton paper, and deep roasted umber, avoiding cold digital grays and high-stress synthetic blues:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Page / Canvas: Warm unbleached paper `#FFF4E8`.
  - Content Sheet / Writing Desk Surface: Crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` providing optimal contrast and focus.
  - Subdued Panel Fill: Warm neutral tint `#FAF0E4`.
- **Text & Content Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Text: Deep warm brown `#713D32` delivering strong, accessible contrast while avoiding the stark hostility of pure `#000000`.
  - Secondary / Supporting Text: Grounded slate `#475569` for meta-labels, timestamps, and secondary descriptions.
  - Muted Borders / Outline Controls: Architectural slate `#64748B` with optical opacity levels for inputs, dividers, and framing.
- **Brand & Action Intent:**
  - Brand Accent: Terracotta `#C65B43` for focal highlights, passive states, and decorative badges.
  - Interactive Action & Primary CTAs: Deep brick terracotta `#A84733` for accessible, decisive touch targets.
  - Gentle Accent & Highlights: Warm golden honey `#E4AB56` used sparingly for AI insight callouts, guided prompts, and quiet encouragement.
- **System States:**
  - Success: Deep forest `#166534` over pale mint `#F0FDF4`.
  - Warning / Gentle Caution: Rich amber `#92400E` over warm cream `#FFFBEB`.
  - Error: Deep crimson `#B91C1C` over soft blush `#FEF2F2`.
  - Information / Reassurance: Deep classic navy `#1E40AF` over soft blue `#EFF6FF`.

## Typography

The typography uses **Be Vietnam Pro** across all touchpoints, taking advantage of its balanced proportions, open apertures, and natural Vietnamese diacritic rendering.

- **Reading Comfort:** Long-form writing and student-caregiver dialogue utilize generous line-heights (`1.65` to `1.75` ratio on body copy) to alleviate reading fatigue and foster contemplative pacing.
- **Editorial Distinction:** Section titles and conversational prompts adopt moderate semi-bold weights rather than heavy black styles, sustaining a warm, humanistic presence rather than an imposing corporate authority.
- **Scale Continuity:** Letter-spacing stays tightly clamped on headlines (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to retain organic warmth, while metadata labels use slightly expanded spacing for rapid, legible scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial baseline arranged in an intentional, centered-sheet canvas system that mirrors physical paper on a desk:

- **Adaptive Structure:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Centered workspace container capped at a comfortable maximum reading width (780px for focused single-author flows; 1140px for split perspective view showing caregiver and student framing side-by-side). Uses 12-column layout with 24px gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px safe margins. Secondary panels collapse into bottom drawers or sequential tabs.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins and gutters. Toolbars dock to the bottom thumb zone to preserve visibility for text entry.
- **Atmospheric Rhythms:** Generous internal padding within cards (`space-xl` on desktop, `space-lg` on mobile) ensures thoughts and assistive cues never feel cramped or urgent.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast dropshadows, synthetic 3D skeuomorphism, and blurred frosted glass. Depth is communicated through tactile paper layers and gentle warm ambient illumination:

- **Level 0 (Desk Canvas):** The warm `#FFF4E8` base backdrop. Flat, quiet, continuous.
- **Level 1 (Paper Document / Content Sheet):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces sitting atop the canvas, defined by a 1px solid stroke in `#64748B` at 18% opacity, accompanied by a soft, warm diffuse shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(113, 61, 50, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Suggestion & Perspective Cards):** White paper elevated slightly during drafting or active inspection: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(113, 61, 50, 0.08)`, 1px outline in `#64748B` at 25% opacity.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Voluntary Decision Dialogues):** Deep warm scrim (`rgba(113, 61, 50, 0.35)`) paired with a paper surface supported by `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(113, 61, 50, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness (`roundedness: 2`) reminiscent of bookbinding corners, notebooks, and smoothed craft stationery:

- **Controls & Small Badges (`rounded` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Text fields, buttons, and status tags retain structural shape with gently softened corners.
- **Cards & Content Sheets (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Writing containers, prompt cards, and AI comparison cards convey hospitality without turning into playful candy-like bubbles.
- **Contextual Popovers & Floating Actions (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Privacy callouts and expression prompts maintain soft containment.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Capsule/pill buttons are avoided for primary actions to distinguish the platform from fast-casual consumer apps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#A84733` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height 44px (touch-friendly), corner radius 8px (`rounded`). Hover state transitions smoothly to `#C65B43`. Focus ring uses `#E4AB56` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary / Supporting Action:** Transparent background with 1px border in `#64748B` (40% opacity), text in `#713D32`. Hover fills with `#FAF0E4`.
- **Gentle / Text Button:** Zero border, text in `#713D32`, subtle underline on hover, zero ambient shadow. Used for voluntary dismissals ("Để sau", "Chưa sẵn sàng chia sẻ").

### Input Fields & Writing Slate
- **Distraction-Free Editor:** Large multi-line text areas use pure `#FFFFFF` background, `#713D32` text, and a quiet 1px border in `#64748B` at 30% opacity.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#C65B43` with a soft outer ring in `rgba(198, 91, 67, 0.15)`. No harsh browser defaults.
- **Placeholder:** Styled in `#475569` at 65% opacity with an inviting, conversational prompt style (e.g., *"Bạn muốn người kia hiểu cảm xúc nào nhất lúc này?"*).

### Perspective & Suggestion Cards (AI Expressive Assistance)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with an intentional left edge highlight in `#E4AB56` (3px border-left accent).
- Contains clear attribution stating: *"Gợi ý cách diễn đạt — Bạn luôn có toàn quyền chỉnh sửa hoặc bỏ qua."*
- Actions inside cards prioritize autonomy: "Áp dụng thử", "Làm dịu giọng điệu", and "Tự viết theo ý mình".

### Chips & Tags
- **Empathy & Tone Indicators:** `#FAF0E4` background, `#713D32` typography, border 1px solid `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.2)`. Padding: 4px 12px, radius 8px.
- **Active State:** `#C65B43` background with white text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control: 20x20px, 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Border 1.5px solid `#64748B`.
- Selected state: Fill `#A84733` with white checkmark/dot.
- Focus indicator: 2px soft halo in `#E4AB56`.

### Privacy & Safe Sharing Banner
- High-trust component using `#EFF6FF` background, `#1E40AF` text, and a clean shield icon.
- Communicates end-to-end user custody explicitly: content is not shared with schools, parents, or outside systems unless the writer explicitly signs off.
